The Interoperable Master Format (IMF) User Group (UG) is a forum for the worldwide community of users and implementers of the [IMF family of standards](https://www.smpte.org/standards/st2067), including content owners, service providers, content platforms, technology vendors, educational institutions and independent developers.

[Read more what is IMF and how it works!](/explainer/)

The objective of the UG is to facilitate and promote the use of IMF worldwide, both on-prem and in the cloud; in coordination with other organizations; and in conjunction with other component-based workflow technologies.

To fulfill its objective, the UG:

-   hosts regular member meetings;
-   organizes public events;
-   collects requirements;
-   develops best practices and technical reports;
-   conducts interoperability testing; and
-   creates promotional and educational materials.

The IMF UG is organized under the umbrella of the [SMPTE](https://www.smpte.org/).
