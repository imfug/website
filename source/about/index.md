<p>The Interoperable Master Format (IMF) User Group (UG) is a forum for the worldwide community of users and implementers of the <a href="https://www.smpte.org/standards/st2067">IMF family of standards</a>, including content owners, service providers, content platforms, technology vendors, educational institutions and independent developers.</p>
<p><a href="/explainer/">Read more what is IMF and how it works!</a></p>
<p>The objective of the UG is to facilitate and promote the use of IMF worldwide, both on-prem and in the cloud; in coordination with other organizations; and in conjunction with other component-based workflow technologies.</p>
<p>To fulfill its objective, the UG:</p>
<ul>
<li>hosts regular member meetings;</li>
<li>organizes public events;</li>
<li>collects requirements;</li>
<li>develops best practices and technical reports;</li>
<li>conducts interoperability testing; and</li>
<li>creates promotional and educational materials.</li>
</ul>
<p>The IMF UG is organized under the umbrella of the <a href="https://www.smpte.org/">SMPTE</a>.</p>
