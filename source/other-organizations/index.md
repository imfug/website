The following lists other industry organizations who speak IMF.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| [EBU IMF-TV](https://tech.ebu.ch/imf) | Help EBU Members better understand the application, advantages and challenges of IMF. |

| [DPP Mastering Format Working Group](https://www.thedpp.com/news/dpp-guidance-makes-imf-implementation-easier) | Applies IMF to broadcast and online domains. |
