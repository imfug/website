<p style="text-align: center;"><em>Making movies is costly and time-consuming. Preserving them is critical.</em></p>
The Interoperable Master Format (IMF) is a recognized and standardized intermediate format for making and distributing complex audio-visual content, and has emerged as a promising candidate for archiving and preserving it.
The HPA IMF User Group (<a href="/">https://www.imfug.com</a>) is organizing two workshops this fall to explore this complex problem:
<ul>
 	<li>Preserving movies with IMF</li>
</ul>
When: September 13 in Amsterdam, NL
Program chair:&nbsp;Siegfried Foessel &lt;<a href="mailto:siegfried.foessel@iis.fraunhofer.de">siegfried.foessel@iis.fraunhofer.de</a>&gt;
Event page:&nbsp;<a href="/events/2019-09-13-workshop-preserving-movies-with-imf/">Workshop on preserving movies with IMF</a>
<ul>
 	<li>Preserving and re-purposing studio productions using IMF</li>
</ul>
When: October 25 in Los Angeles, CA, USA
Program chair: Florian Schleich &lt;<a href="mailto:fschleich@netflix.com">fschleich@netflix.com</a>&gt;
Event page:&nbsp;<a href="/events/2019-10-workshop-on-preserving-and-re-purposing-studio-productions-using-imf/">Workshop on preserving and re-purposing studio productions using IMF</a>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong><em>Submit your presentation proposals by July 1 at&nbsp;<a href="mailto:archive@imfug.com">archive@imfug.com</a></em></strong></p>
Submissions from archives, content producers/owners, product manufacturers and movie professionals are welcome. All submissions should include a short abstract (3-4 sentences) and specify the target event (Amsterdam and/or Los Angeles). Presentations should have a maximum duration of 15 minutes and should focus on archival and preservation use cases and workflows.
These workshops are a unique opportunity to meet experts and implementers, and share ideas, use cases, and requirements. Admission will be open to all, with tickets available starting August 1, 2019.
