<p>Join a panel of experts on June 17, 2024 for a discussion on <strong>improving QC efficiency across the supply chain</strong> using EBU QC cards and the Interoperable Master Format (IMF)!</p>
<p><a href="https://ebu-ch.zoom.us/meeting/register/tJcpcOCqqT0tEtHft7D4fdNT9tDDtlrYEo2G"><strong><mark style="background-color:#f9ff00">REGISTER TODAY!</mark></strong></a></p>
<p>This webinar will consist of presentations by panelists followed by a Q&amp;A session driven by the audience. It is a follow-up to our <a href="/workshop-report-better-qc-using-imf-ibc-2023/">IMF UG workshop held at IBC 2023</a>, which highlighted the <strong>urgent need to avoid repeated QC rejections based on false positives</strong>. IMF is the worldwide standard for the interchange of component-based studio masters and EBU QC is a standardized language for expressing QC reports.</p>
<p>This free webinar is made possible by a collaboration of the <a href="http://tech.ebu.ch/">EBU</a> and the <a href="/">IMF UG</a>.</p>
