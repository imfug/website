Join a panel of experts on June 17, 2024 for a discussion on **improving QC efficiency across the supply chain** using EBU QC cards and the Interoperable Master Format (IMF)!

[**REGISTER TODAY!**](https://ebu-ch.zoom.us/meeting/register/tJcpcOCqqT0tEtHft7D4fdNT9tDDtlrYEo2G)

This webinar will consist of presentations by panelists followed by a Q&A session driven by the audience. It is a follow-up to our [IMF UG workshop held at IBC 2023](/workshop-report-better-qc-using-imf-ibc-2023/), which highlighted the **urgent need to avoid repeated QC rejections based on false positives**. IMF is the worldwide standard for the interchange of component-based studio masters and EBU QC is a standardized language for expressing QC reports.

This free webinar is made possible by a collaboration of the [EBU](http://tech.ebu.ch/) and the [IMF UG](/).
