---
title: The IMF User Group joins SMPTE
date: '2024-03-07 09:36:03'
categories:
  - Announcement
---

After many years under the steadfast stewardship of the [Hollywood Professional Association](https://hpaonline.com/), we will now be operating as the first, of hopefully many, user group under the umbrella of [SMPTE](https://www.smpte.org), the home of the [Interoperable Master Format (IMF)](https://www.smpte.org/standards/st2067) standard.

Our mission and activities remain unchanged: to make IMF responsive to the needs of the community.
