We are hosting a fall plugfest on November 19, 2026 in Pasadena, CA, USA,
co-located with the [SMPTE MTS](https://summit.smpte.org/2026).

The following test vectors have been submitted:

* [SMPTE ST 2067-201 IAB Level 0 Plug-in revision (3ED)](https://pub.smpte.org/doc/st2067-201/)
* MXF track files packaged using [regmxflib](https://github.com/sandflow/regmxflib)
* [SMPTE ST 2067-204 (ADM in IMF)](https://pub.smpte.org/doc/st2067-204/)
* Timed text for Subtitles, Hearing Impaired Captions, Visually Impaired Text,
  Commentary, Karaoke and Forced Narrative

Plugfest are open to IMF UG members and invited guests.

**[Join today!](/join)**

A plugfest is a unique opportunity to gain implementation experience with IMF and network with the members of the community. Read our explainer at [https://www.imfug.com/plugfest/](/plugfest/).
