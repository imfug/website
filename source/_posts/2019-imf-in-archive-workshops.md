[**Preserving movies with the Interoperable Master Format (IMF)**](https://imf-in-archives-ibc-2019.eventbrite.com) _13 September 2019 at the Eye in Amsterdam, NL_ Program chair: Siegfried Foessel [**Preserving and re-purposing studio productions using IMF**](https://imf-in-archive-atc-2019.eventbrite.com) _25 October 2019 in Los Angeles, USA_ Program chair: Florian Schleich
