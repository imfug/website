## Proceedings

[Video of the event](https://youtu.be/P_KjL5SRhqE) _Thanks to [Ownzones](https://ownzones.com/) for the recording and editing!_

## When

14 Sep 2019 from 12:30 PM to 2 PM

## Where

Emerald room at the RAI

## Description

Learn about IMF through:

-   an overview of the use cases and technology underlying the IMF standard
-   a series of tutorials using some of the latest products on the market, in the cloud and on-premise

The presentations will be followed by 20 minutes of Q&A.

## Registration

No registration necessary. The session is free to all IBC attendees.
